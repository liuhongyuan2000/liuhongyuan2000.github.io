PROJECT 01 / AI FOR SCIENCE
ICLR 2026 · ORAL

RealPDEBench

Bridging the gap between simulated physics and reality.

建立数值模拟与真实物理世界之间的联系。

An evaluation benchmark for learning dynamical systems from paired experimental measurements and simulations. Five physical scenarios encompass cylinder wakes, controlled flows, fluid–structure coupling, foils and combustion.

利用实验测量与数值模拟配对数据，构建面向真实物理系统的科学机器学习评测基准；覆盖圆柱绕流、受控流动、流固耦合、翼型和燃烧五类场景。

PROJECT 02 / COMPUTATIONAL SCIENCE
WAICA 2026 · MAIN CONFERENCE

RSPMA

Predicting where computation will matter next.

预测下一时刻计算资源应该集中在哪里。

Predictive mesh adaptation for autoregressive graph-based PDE rollout. A future-aware monitor predictor, stable current–future fusion, and topology-preserving motion work together to improve long-horizon numerical prediction.

面向图结构 PDE 求解器的预测式网格自适应方法。通过未来监测场预测、当前—未来信息融合及拓扑保持的网格移动，改善长时间递推预测的稳定性。

PROJECT 03 / EXPERIMENTAL FLUID DYNAMICS
JOURNAL OF FLUID MECHANICS · 2026

When vortices meet forcing

Unraveling the competition between self-excitation and external forcing.

揭示自激振动与外部受迫运动之间的竞争机制。

In a forced, elastically mounted circular cylinder, we identified dual-frequency oscillation, frequency-switching and resonant synchronization. The study combines structural response, flow visualization and hydrodynamic analysis.

研究受迫条件下弹性支承圆柱的涡激振动，识别双频响应、频率切换与共振同步三种动力学区间，并结合结构响应、流场可视化与水动力分析探究机理。
